# October plan V2.1: catalog by states, one product, the gift as a modality

Date: 2026-09-23. Decided by Óscar and Liliana while reviewing `../Alta_Vibracion_729_Plan_Ejecucion_2309.md` (plan V2.1, which replaces the V1 file). The plan is the spec; this record keeps the choices it left open.

## Decisions

- **Only YO-01 sells.** The catalog (`lib/catalog.ts`) carries a status per service: `active`, `paused`, `expectation`, `draft`. The type forces a price and a duration on `active`/`paused` and forbids them on the rest. `createBooking` rejects anything that is not `active`, whatever the form sent. YO-02 is renamed "Mi Camino 729", shown in preparation, without its old price. YO-03 and the Nosotros, Celebremos and Empresas lines are in preparation and capture interest only.
- **The gift is a modality of YO-01**, not a service. `allowsGift` on the service; the separate `reg-01` id is gone (it never produced a booking, `createBooking` refused it). Gift orders will get their own table when they ship, because a paid voucher has no slot and `bookings.starts_at` is not nullable.
- **Public routes for the new pages use the plan's Spanish slugs** (`/yo`, `/nosotros`, `/celebremos`, `/empresas`, `/regalar`, `/encuentros/[codigo]`): they go on QR codes and campaign links, and Liliana reads them aloud at events. The existing English routes stay. This is a deliberate exception to the "routes in English" convention.
- **Postgres stays the commercial record for October.** Interests, campaigns and gift orders go in the same Neon database with a CSV export for Liliana. Airtable is a possible later sync, never a dependency of the sale.
- **No mockups.** The plan refers to an interactive mockup that is not in the workspace; the pages are built on the existing brand components and the plan's copy.
- **Match is a placeholder.** `/nosotros/match` uses the expectation template and links nowhere; the demo (`alta-code`) will be its own app.
- **Payment instructions will show a Bre-B key**, from configuration, never a bank account number on a page.
- **Campaign eligibility** = campaign active and the contact matches an interest registered for that campaign before activation. A pending promo order holds its promo cupo for the same 24 hours as its slot.
- **Gift capacity** is an admin warning (paid vouchers without a slot against free slots in the next 30 days), never a hard block. Redemption has two paths: the beneficiary enters the voucher code on `/agenda`, or Liliana creates the booking by hand from the admin.
- **Admin editing is MVP.** The handoff document must list what Liliana changes without code and what still needs a code change.
- **Deferred:** the post-session survey, the newsletter, automated voucher delivery, the payment gateway.

## Delivery order (plan section 12)

1. Catalog by states, navigation, gift visible in four places (this record's PR), then interest capture with its table and admin lists.
2. Campaigns "Encuentro 729": table, `/encuentros/[codigo]`, eligibility and promo cupos, price frozen on the order.
3. Agenda and payment: Bre-B key in the instructions, 30-day horizon, manual booking from the admin.
4. Gifts: `gift_orders`, voucher code, redemption on `/agenda`, capacity warning.
5. Handoff: CSV export, pause offers, operating guide for Liliana.
